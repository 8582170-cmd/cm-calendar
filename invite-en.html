📨 Invitation page

What is the event?

The event link becomes an invitation page: the invitation, the clock, venue details with Waze, add-to-calendar, RSVP, reminder sign-up and a blessings wall. Works with a saved address, and is saved with it.

Background and music (optional)

Video and music are kept for a week; if the invitation page is not activated by then, they are deleted. Video: up to 20MB, short (10–30 seconds) and silent, plays in a loop. Music: up to 10MB. Guests start it with a tap (browsers don’t allow autoplay). Only upload files you are allowed to use.

What else to turn on

🔒 Automatic reminders, thank-you email, gallery and card gifts work with the full package.

Paste a whole list, or import from Excel / CSV

One line per guest, with commas: name, email (and/or phone), group, number of guests. For example: Cohen family, cohen@gmail.com, Groom’s family, 4. You can also paste columns from Excel.

FAQ

How do I send the invitation?

After saving, add your guests and send by email, SMS, voice message or WhatsApp: each guest gets a personal link with their name.

How do I see who RSVP’d?

In the Tracking tab you see how many are coming, adults and kids, and the full list. You can also download it to Excel. Management works from the browser where the address was saved.

Who sees the blessings?

Whoever writes a blessing chooses if everyone sees it on the wall, or only the hosts. You can hide or delete any blessing, and print them all as a book of blessings.

How does the Tehillim division work?

Each guest picks chapters out of the 150 chapters of Tehillim, writes their name, and can sign up for an email reminder. Taken chapters are marked, and when the whole book is taken a new round starts.

And how do I receive gifts?

Paste your own payment link, like Bit, PayBox or a payment page, and a “Gift” button appears on the invitation page. The money goes straight to you.

Is it free?

Yes. Only uploading a photo requires an account, which is also free.
