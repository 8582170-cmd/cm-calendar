Questions

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. Build the whole calendar for the whole year with every option. You can download one month free in the full version, and once, a simple calendar for the whole year (up to 3 zmanim, 10 photos and 25 events).

How do Hebrew birthdays and yahrzeits work?

Click a day and choose a birthday, anniversary or yahrzeit. Each one repeats every year on its Hebrew date (or English date, if you prefer), so it lands on the right day automatically, every year.

Which cities and customs are supported?

About 150 cities in Israel and around the world, including New York, Brooklyn, Lakewood, Los Angeles, Toronto, London, Paris and Antwerp. You can also set an exact location. Choose Ashkenazi, Sephardi, Chabad or the custom common in communities abroad.

Is it an Israel or a diaspora calendar?

Both. For places outside Israel the calendar follows the diaspora: two days of Yom Tov and the diaspora Torah portion schedule.

How do I print?

You download a PDF in the size you chose. Print at home, or send it to a print shop for spiral binding.

Is everything in English?

The simple builder and the countdown builder are in English, and so is the printed calendar: month names, holidays, Torah portions and zmanim. The full builder with advanced settings is currently in Hebrew.
